Build a complete, functional Android camera application named "Ansari Pixel Camera AI". MAIN GOAL: Create an offline-first, AI-powered camera app inspired by the natural portrait photography and computational photography of Google Pixel phones. The app must capture photos, automatically enhance them, and produce realistic DSLR-style portrait results without requiring an internet connection. IMPORTANT: Build a working Android application, not just a UI prototype or design mockup. 1. CAMERA ACCESS AND PHOTO CAPTURE - Request camera permission when the app is first opened. - Use the phone's camera to capture photos. - Provide a modern, full-screen camera preview. - Include a large, easy-to-use shutter button. - Include front and rear camera switching. - Support flash on/off/auto when supported by the device. - Support portrait photography and autofocus when available. - Allow the user to select existing photos from the gallery. 2. AUTOMATIC PHOTO ENHANCEMENT Immediately after a photo is captured, automatically process it using on-device image processing and AI models. Target processing time: 5–10 seconds on supported devices. If processing takes longer, show a progress indicator instead of freezing or crashing. Automatically perform: - Natural face enhancement. - Improved exposure and brightness. - Natural contrast and color correction. - Noise reduction, especially in low-light photos. - Sharpening of facial details without excessive smoothing. - Improved white balance and natural skin tones. - Background segmentation and realistic portrait blur. - Edge refinement around hair, ears, shoulders, and clothing. - Preserve original image resolution as much as possible. 3. STRICT FACE IDENTITY PRESERVATION This is the most important requirement. - Never replace, regenerate, reshape, or reconstruct the person's face. - Do not change facial identity, eye shape, nose, lips, beard, hairstyle, expression, or skin tone. - Do not create a different person or an AI-generated replacement face. - Preserve the original facial structure and recognizable details. - Enhance only the real pixels captured by the camera. - Avoid artificial beauty filters, plastic skin, and excessive smoothing. - Keep the person's natural appearance. 4. REALISTIC DSLR-STYLE BACKGROUND BLUR Create a natural portrait effect inspired by computational photography on Google Pixel phones. - Keep the subject sharp and the background softly blurred. - Detect the person separately from the background. - Apply realistic depth-like blur to the background. - Preserve fine hair details and avoid cutting off ears or shoulders. - Avoid blurring the person's face, body, or clothing. - Use adjustable blur strength if supported. - Make the result look natural rather than artificial. If the phone does not support true depth capture, estimate the subject mask using an on-device segmentation model and apply a high-quality blur algorithm. 5. FULLY OFFLINE OPERATION The app must work without Wi-Fi, mobile data, or an internet connection after installation. - Use on-device image processing. - Bundle compatible AI models with the application. - Do not require cloud APIs, online AI services, or remote servers. - Do not require account registration or login. - Do not upload photographs to external servers. - Store photos locally on the device. - Make all core camera and enhancement features available offline. Use lightweight, compatible on-device technologies such as MediaPipe Image Segmenter, TensorFlow Lite, LiteRT, or OpenCV where appropriate. Select only libraries and models that are compatible with the project's Android version and licensing requirements. 6. USER INTERFACE Create a premium, minimal, modern interface. Design: - Black and dark-grey background. - White text and subtle silver accents. - Clean camera controls. - Smooth, lightweight animations. - Large shutter button. - Small gallery preview. - Simple settings screen. - No unnecessary advertisements or clutter. - No excessive visual effects that slow down the phone. 7. AFTER-CAPTURE SCREEN After taking a photo: - Automatically begin enhancement. - Show a clear processing indicator. - Display the enhanced photo when processing finishes. - Provide a before-and-after comparison slider. - Add buttons for Save, Retake, Share, and Edit. - Save the enhanced photo in the device gallery. - Preserve the original photo unless the user chooses to replace or delete it. 8. MANUAL CONTROLS Include optional controls for: - Background blur strength. - Brightness. - Contrast. - Sharpness. - Warmth. - Natural enhancement intensity. Automatic enhancement should be enabled by default. 9. PERFORMANCE AND DEVICE COMPATIBILITY The application must be optimized for budget Android phones as well as more powerful devices. - Detect device capabilities. - Use lightweight models and optimized image processing. - Avoid loading unnecessarily large models into memory. - Process photos on a background thread. - Keep the camera preview responsive. - Handle insufficient memory gracefully. - Provide a fallback enhancement method if an AI model cannot run. - Do not promise 5–10-second processing on every device; optimize for that target and report progress when needed. - Use Android CameraX where compatible. - Support the project's configured minimum Android SDK. - Handle all runtime permissions correctly. 10. PROJECT AND BUILD REQUIREMENTS Generate a complete, buildable Android Studio project using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Include: - All required source code. - Gradle configuration and dependencies. - AndroidManifest.xml with correct permissions. - CameraX integration. - On-device image segmentation and enhancement pipeline. - Required model files and their licenses. - Gallery access and local photo saving. - Error handling and loading indicators. - Instructions to build and export a signed APK. Do not provide only pseudocode, incomplete snippets, or a visual prototype. Before finishing, verify that the project compiles, camera permissions work, photos can be captured, offline enhancement works, and the app can save the processed photo. If any requested feature cannot be implemented with the selected model or device, explain the limitation and provide the best functional offline alternative. FINAL PRIORITY: Natural photographs, unchanged facial identity, realistic DSLR-style background blur, fully offline processing, and reliable operation on Android phones.
